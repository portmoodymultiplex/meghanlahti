About Meghan

This town is my life's work.

Port Moody is a passion for me. I've spent most of my adult life in service to this city.

The Story

How I got here.

I first ran for Port Moody City Council in 1996. My campaign sign just said “I love this town.” Three decades on, that feeling hasn't changed. In October of 2022, Port Moody elected its first female mayor.

One of the things I'm most proud of from my early years on council is the work we did to create Bert Flinn Park. Beginning in 1996, I championed the trade-off that set aside that 311-acre stretch of mountainside as a protected park, in exchange for concentrating density in Inlet Centre. Voters approved it by referendum in 1999. It was controversial at the time, but it gave us neighbourhoods that are now widely loved, and a nature park I will keep working to protect.

The last four years have been some of the most rewarding of my time in public service. This council adopted a strategic plan that nearly all of us could agree on, and we followed through on it. We brought in the city's first Land Management Strategy. We held two Youth Summits. We started the Mayor's Task Force on Arts and Business. The Shoreline Shuttle is coming back this summer, the multi-use path on St. Johns is built, and the e-bike and e-scooter program for the last mile is up and running.

All of that came out of a real love for this community, and a pride in being part of how it has grown. That feeling has only deepened, and it's what I bring with me to every council meeting and every conversation on a doorstep.

First elected to council.

Family & Community

The rest of her life happens here too.

Meghan and her husband have raised their family in Port Moody. Outside of council, her career was nearly twenty years in post-secondary education, working as an educator, then a College Director, then a Provincial Director responsible for hundreds of employees. And on top of all that, she's been the kind of mom who shows up, year after year, to the things her kids and a lot of other people's kids cared about.
