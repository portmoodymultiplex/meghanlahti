BC campaign finance rules

When donations open, here is how it will work.

The rules, in plain terms.

  • Maximum contribution: $1,429.70 per individual for 2026. Updated by Elections BC every January 1st.
  • Donations accepted only from individuals. Corporations, unions, and other organizations cannot contribute.
  • You must be a BC resident and either a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident.
  • You must use your own funds. Donations made on behalf of someone else are prohibited under the Local Elections Campaign Financing Act.
  • All contributions are reported to Elections BC after the election, as required by law.

For the full rules, see the Elections BC Local Candidates resource page. Questions about a specific donation can go to anthony@lahti2026.com.
