Get involved

Help shape Port Moody’s next chapter.

A city is shaped by the people who show up. We would love it if that is you, in whatever way fits your life.

You do not need political experience or a lot of free time. A couple of hours, and the sense that this town is worth showing up for, is honestly enough. Pick something from the list, or just tell us you want to help and we will find something that fits.

Ways to help

  • Knock on doors with a partner
  • Deliver campaign literature in your area
  • Host a small conversation with friends
  • Request a lawn sign, or help deliver them
  • Lend a hand at events
  • Make a few calls from home
  • Share a personal endorsement
  • Help behind the scenes
