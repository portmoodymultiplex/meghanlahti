Volunteer.

Cities are shaped by the people who decide to show up. We'd love it if that's you.

There's a Place for You

Pick whatever fits your life.

You don't need political experience or a lot of time. A couple of hours and the feeling that this city is worth showing up for is honestly enough. Have a look at the options below, or just tell us you want to help and we'll find something that works for you.

Door Knocking
Knock on doors with us.

It's the most effective thing you can do in a municipal race. We'll pair you up with a partner, hand you a walk list, and walk you through what to say. Shifts run from summer right through to election day.

Phone Banking
Make a few calls from your couch.

Calling supporters, reminding people about advance voting, checking in on the folks who pledged. It's especially helpful in the last two weeks of the race.

Signs
Put a sign up. Help a neighbour put one up too.

Help us install and look after lawn signs around the city once the campaign officially launches.

Events
Lend a hand at events.

Town halls, community events, the campaign launch. If you like being around people, this is a good fit.

Think Tank
Come to a Thursday session. Bring a few friends.

Small-group conversations with Meghan about the issues people are actually living with. Single moms, seniors, small business owners, young families. The voices that usually get missed are the ones we most want in the room.

Word of Mouth
Talk to your neighbours.

Honestly, this is the simplest and most effective thing on the list. Talk to the people you already know, in your own words, about why this race matters to you.

Ready to jump in?

Fill out the short form on the contact page and someone from the team will be in touch within a day or two.
